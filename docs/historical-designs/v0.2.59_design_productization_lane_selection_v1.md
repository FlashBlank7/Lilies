## Problem

After v0.2 backlog closure, the project had several possible productization lanes but no selected next stage. A deterministic choice was needed so automatic evolution could continue without guessing.

## Boundary

- In scope: score candidate product lanes and choose one next stage.
- In scope: keep externally blocked and governance-blocked lanes out of immediate implementation.
- Out of scope: implementing the selected monitoring product surface.

## Final Design

`scripts/v02_productization_lane_selection.py` scores five lanes using:

```text
evidence + readiness + product_value + next_slice_specificity - external_blocker - safety_governance_blocker
```

The highest unblocked lane is `adaptive_monitoring_product_surface`, with next version `v0.2.60_adaptive_monitoring_product_surface`.

## Acceptance

- Exactly five lanes are evaluated.
- Human panel and governed memory are blocked.
- Monitoring surface wins over E08 extended controls and complexity router rollout.
- The next stage and first workingon file are explicit.

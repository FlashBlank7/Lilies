## Problem

Stage reports were written with inconsistent section names and optional sections, even though they are the only next-stage task authority.

## Boundary

- In scope: canonical template, skill template, validator.
- Out of scope: retroactive migration of all old stage reports.

## Final Design

`docs/stage-reports/STAGE_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md` defines mandatory sections. `scripts/validate_stage_report_template.py` checks required section presence and order. Future stage reports must use explicit `none` rows instead of omitting sections.

- v0.2.62 report validates against the template.

## Problem

E08 extended controls are broad. If E08 is selected, the next version needs a serious but bounded target rather than claiming full sidecar completion.

## Boundary

This design scoped the next E08 slice but did not implement it.

## Final Design

- v0.2.65 should target `e08_policy_controls_surface`.
- The next slice should begin with backend/API policy-control visibility, deterministic control tests, and operator disposition.
- Studio/UI may be included only if the implementation closure remains coherent and testable.
- Full Platform Harness sidecar completion remains out of scope unless enforcement, observability, persistence, cancellation, budget, worker lease, controls, and operational behavior are all closed.

## Acceptance

- v0.2.64 names `v0.2.65_e08_policy_controls_surface` as the next version.
- The next slice is not described as full Platform Harness sidecar completion.
- Remaining E08 breadth is bounded.

## Problem

The full historical test sweep revealed incompatible expectations across time. Without a detector, future automatic evolution may treat old archived assertions as current regressions and stop for the wrong reason.

## Boundary

- Do not rewrite old v0.2 assertions in this stage.
- Do not hide the full-suite failure.
- Classify known conflicts with evidence and owner lane.

## Solution

- Capture known conflict families from the v0.3.14 full-suite sweep.
- Check that at least one current test records the later behavior where applicable.
- Emit structured evidence that distinguishes current gates, historical conflicts, and unresolved unknown failures.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Conflict families include complexity-router default enablement and older builder behavior assertions.
- Evidence says these are diagnostic conflicts, not current v0.3.x release-gate blockers.
- Unknown failures remain blocking in the detector.

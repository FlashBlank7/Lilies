## Problem

v0.2.118 closed the `test_suite` worker offload handler, but remaining Platform Harness sidecar gaps still include worker-owned handlers and operational concerns. The next version must choose one concrete implementation target without reselecting completed work.

## Boundary

- Do not implement the selected slice in v0.2.119.
- Do not reselect completed `test_suite`, `workflow_run`, `scheduler_trigger`, heartbeat registry, handler catalog, stdio/container egress, secret rotation, policy controls, Studio controls, or runbook lifecycle.
- Do not claim full Platform Harness sidecar completion.
- Do not use `docs/workingon/` as the source of next tasks.

## Final Scheme

Add a deterministic selector script that records completed slices, scores remaining candidates, and emits the selected next implementation slice with evidence paths.

## Acceptance

- The selector chooses a concrete next implementation slice.
- Completed test_suite/workflow_run/scheduler slices are preserved as evidence and excluded.
- Full sidecar completion remains explicitly false.
- The next version and first design path are emitted by the selector.

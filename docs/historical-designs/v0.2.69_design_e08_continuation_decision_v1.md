## Problem

E08 had accumulated a policy surface, behavior matrix, and cancellation/budget evidence, but full sidecar completion still had larger product gaps. The project needed a source-linked decision on whether to continue E08 or pause it.

## Boundary

This design decided and recorded the next lane. It did not implement editable controls, runbook, or complexity-router guardrails.

## Final Design

- `scripts/v02_69_e08_continuation_decision.py` compares continuing E08 editable controls, continuing E08 runbook, pausing E08 and moving to complexity-router, and falsely declaring full sidecar complete.
- The selected decision is `pause_e08_move_complexity_router`.
- False full sidecar completion is rejected.

## Acceptance

- A single next lane decision is produced.
- Non-selected E08 continuations are explicitly deferred.
- Tests pass.

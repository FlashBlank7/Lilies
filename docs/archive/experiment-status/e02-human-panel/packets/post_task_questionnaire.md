# E02 post-task questionnaire

Status: prepared_pending_external_execution

Ask the condition-specific questions after each packet and the preference question after both packets.

## After Each Packet

- How confident are you that your repair target is correct? Use 1 to 5.
- What part of the material made the answer easiest to find?
- What part of the material slowed you down?
- Did you need any facilitator clarification? If yes, what was clarified?

## After Both Packets

- Which packet was easier to turn into an actionable repair recommendation: `raw_json`, `readable_testframe`, or `no_preference`?
- Briefly explain the preference.

## Capture Mapping

- Confidence goes into `confidence_1_to_5`.
- Final preference goes into `preference`.
- Clarifications and free-text comments go into `notes`.

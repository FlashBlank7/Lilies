# E02 consent and safety notes

Status: prepared_pending_external_execution

## Consent Script

Participants should be told:

- This is a usability/timing study of two evidence presentation formats.
- Participation is voluntary.
- They may stop at any time.
- The study captures timing, answers, confidence, and non-identifying notes.
- No personal identifiers should be written in the result sheet.

## Privacy

- Use participant ids such as `P001`, not names or email addresses.
- Do not include private repository secrets, customer data, credentials, or personal data in packets.
- Store raw notes in the same controlled repo/document storage as other experiment evidence.

## Safety and Fairness

- Do not frame the task as an evaluation of participant skill.
- Do not pressure participants to be fast.
- Counterbalance packet order to reduce learning-order bias.
- Report facilitator interventions rather than hiding them.

## External Execution Boundary

These notes are preparation material. E02 remains externally blocked until a real panel is run and analyzed.

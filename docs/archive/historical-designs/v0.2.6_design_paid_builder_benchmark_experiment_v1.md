## Design Summary

This design connected real paid Builder Team generation to the deterministic benchmark suite introduced in v0.2.5.

The experiment runs one bounded Builder build, records the generated draft or failure state, evaluates the candidate `WorkflowSpec` through the suite endpoint when possible, writes structured JSON evidence, and produces a concise `.docx` experiment report.

## Result

- Live paid Builder run completed with `needs_attention`.
- Builder usage evidence: `model_call=36`, `tool_call=54`.
- Failure reason: `test_summary_exists` required nonexistent node type `extract_text`.
- Candidate draft had 5 nodes, 4 edges, and 1 test.
- Benchmark suite evaluation succeeded with score `0.85`.

## Remaining Risk

- The experiment exposed a Builder self-consistency defect rather than proving the Builder can reliably reach `ready`.
- DOCX visual render QA was not completed because local `soffice` is unavailable.
- Next stage should fix test required-node/tool self-consistency and rerun the paid benchmark.

## Problem

After the adaptive policy shipped in `v0.2.48`, Lilies still needed at least one bounded paid/live slice proving that adaptive had real Builder value rather than being only a deterministic heuristic.

## Boundary

- In scope: one bounded paid/live comparison on a selected family using the canonical E05 runner.
- In scope: raw JSON, summary Markdown, concise DOCX, and experiment-status updates.
- Out of scope: multi-family sweeps, online learning, or deadline-visibility product work.

## Final Design

- Use `data_analyzer` as the first live-validation family because:
  - the adaptive policy clearly predicts `deep`,
  - `v0.2.47` already showed strong sensitivity to reuse depth on this family,
  - the case is structurally different enough to matter.
- Run `shallow / deep / adaptive` under the same bounded budget.
- Treat adaptive as successful only if it resolves to a concrete depth and produces interpretable live behavior.

## Acceptance

- A live result exists for an adaptive arm inside the canonical E05 runner.
- The report states whether adaptive matches or improves on the best fixed-depth arm for the selected family.
- The stage leaves a reusable evidence chain for ledger/index reads.

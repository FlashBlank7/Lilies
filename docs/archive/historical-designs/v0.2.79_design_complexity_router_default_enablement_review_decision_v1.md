## Problem

Guardrails and bounded live validation passed, but default router behavior remained disabled. The project needed a decision between immediate enablement review, staged rollout preparation, or continued deferral.

## Boundary

This design made the enablement-review path decision only. It did not enable complexity-router defaults or change builder behavior.

## Final Design

- Decision selected `prepare_staged_rollout`.
- Immediate enablement review was deferred until staged rollout preparation exists and frontend verification is restored or explicitly waived.
- Continued deferral was rejected because it wastes completed guardrail and live evidence.
- `default_enabled=false` remains unchanged.

## Acceptance

- Decision evidence selects one option.
- Tests verify selected and rejected alternatives.
- Defaults remain disabled.

## Design Summary

Builder benchmark v1 has been upgraded from a single-case structural scorer into a suite-level evaluator.

The suite evaluates multiple `BuilderBenchmarkCase` entries, aggregates score and pass rate, reports failed cases, records optional baseline trend deltas, carries cost metadata, and exposes the operation through a Platform Harness `benchmark` task.

## Remaining Risk

- The suite is deterministic and evaluates supplied candidates only.
- Real Builder Team generation quality still needs a bounded paid/live experiment.
- Benchmark history is not durable yet; trend baselines are request-provided.

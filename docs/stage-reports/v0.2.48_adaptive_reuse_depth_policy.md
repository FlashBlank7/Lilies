## Stage Answer

`v0.2.48` converts the E05 policy insight into code: Lilies can now answer template suggestions with an explicit `effective_reuse_depth`, a concrete `recommended_action`, and a readable `policy_reason`.

The first adaptive rule is intentionally narrow and evidence-backed:

- if no credible template match exists, stay at `none`,
- if the matched template requires visible complex dataflow bricks such as `parameter_extractor`, prefer `deep`,
- otherwise prefer `shallow`.

The deterministic backtest is good enough for this stage: it matches the best known family outcome exactly for `code_reviewer` and `data_analyzer`, and stays within the known success envelope for `customer_support_router`.

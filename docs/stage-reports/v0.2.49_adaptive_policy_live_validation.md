## Stage Answer

`v0.2.49` answers the most important near-term question positively: the adaptive policy is not just a deterministic paper rule. In a real `data_analyzer` Builder run, adaptive resolved to `deep`, concretized BuildPlan reuse depth to `deep`, published successfully, and finished faster than both explicit `shallow` and explicit `deep`.

For this slice:

- `shallow`: `published`, `213.959s`, `9 / 20` model/tool calls
- `deep`: `published`, `301.290s`, `15 / 23` model/tool calls
- `adaptive`: `published`, `159.669s`, `11 / 17` model/tool calls, resolved to `deep`

This is strong evidence that the adaptive rule has real operational value, but it is still single-family evidence, not a blanket defaultization license.

## 6. Paid/Live Result

Evidence:

- Raw JSON: `docs/experiment-status/evidence/experiment_v0.2.41_e05_success_condition_2026_07_09.json`
- Chart: `docs/experiment-status/evidence/experiment_v0.2.41_e05_success_condition_summary.png`

Provider/model:

- Provider: `multi`
- Generator model: `deepseek-v4-pro`
- Runtime model: `deepseek-v4-pro`

| Depth | Build status | BuildPlan reuse_depth | Elapsed | Model/tool calls | Template expand | Benchmark | Failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `none` | `needs_attention` | `none` | `437.063s` | `27 / 45` | `0` | score `0.85`, pass_rate `1.0` | `model stream timed out after 180s`; `task_failure.type=model_provider`; `timeout_like=true` |
| `shallow` | `published` | `shallow` | `123.267s` | `20 / 30` | `1` | score `0.85`, pass_rate `1.0` | none |
| `deep` | `ready` | `deep` | `331.196s` | `47 / 57` | `1` | score `0.85`, pass_rate `1.0` | none |

Conclusion:

- `shallow` is the best supported current operating point for this code-review Template-friendly case.
- `deep` can succeed but is materially more expensive in this run.
- `none` failed through an auditable provider stream timeout; benchmark pass does not equal Builder success because the build remained `needs_attention` and generated no tests.
- Original E05 remains open because this is a single requirement family and `none` did not produce a clean quality/cost comparison.

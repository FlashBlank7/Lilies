## 6. Experiment Result

Evidence:

- Raw JSON: `docs/experiment-status/evidence/experiment_v0.2.38_e05_template_reuse_depth_2026_07_09.json`
- Cost chart: `docs/experiment-status/evidence/experiment_v0.2.38_e05_template_reuse_depth_calls.png`
- DOCX: `docs/experiment-status/reports/2026-07-09_2051_E05_template_reuse_depth_live_comparison.docx`

| Depth | Build status | BuildPlan reuse_depth | Elapsed | Model/tool calls | Template suggestions/expands | Benchmark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `none` | `published` | `none` | `228.812s` | `24 / 41` | `1 / 0` | score `0.85`, pass_rate `1.0` |
| `shallow` | `needs_attention` | `shallow` | `385.149s` | `42 / 73` | `1 / 2` | score `0.733`, pass_rate `0.0` |
| `deep` | `needs_attention` | `deep` | `412.285s` | `42 / 77` | `1 / 2` | score `0.733`, pass_rate `0.0` |

Conclusion:

- `none` was the only arm to reach `published`, and it had the lowest cost.
- `shallow` and `deep` did change Builder behavior: both called `template_suggestions` and `template_expand`.
- The current reuse-depth path is not yet a stable quality improvement. In this case, deeper reuse triggered expansion, removal, and repair loops but did not pass mandatory tests.
- This experiment should not be marked `已应用` until the engineering fix lands and is rerun.
